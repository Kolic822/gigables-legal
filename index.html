Privacy Policy

Your gigs, fees, expenses and personal notes stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and we cannot read them. We can only access the small amount of data needed for usernames, invitations and moderation.

Gigables is run by Teo Kolić, Croatia ("we"), who is the data controller. Contact: gigables.support@gmail.com.

If you never create a username, we hold no data about you unless you email us.

If you add someone's name or phone number as a gig contact and share that gig, members will see it. Only do this if that person would expect it.

When you send past gigs to a friend, they get a one-time copy of the details you chose, including fees only if you switch that on. We cannot read it, and the friend keeps what they add.

What the app asks to access

Each of these is optional, and you can turn it off in iOS Settings.

  • Calendar: adds your gigs to Apple Calendar and keeps them updated.
  • Notifications: payment and gig day reminders, and friend, band and gig updates.
  • Photos: saves images and videos you choose to save. The app does not read your library.
  • Alarms: rings before soundcheck.
  • Camera: scans a friend's QR code. Nothing from the camera is saved or sent.

Place searches go to Apple Maps. Google Maps or Waze open only if you choose them, and they then handle your data under their own policies.

Gigables has no advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not sell your data.

Apple runs iCloud, push notifications and Apple Maps for the app, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area under its own safeguards. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with Apple's iCloud protections. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your Apple Account protected with two-factor authentication.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a portable copy, or object to how we use it. You can withdraw consent at any time, and you can delete your profile in Settings. To use the other rights, email us.

You can complain to the Croatian data protection authority, AZOP, or the authority in your country.

Age limit

Usernames, friends and bands are for people aged 16 or over. If you believe someone younger has a profile, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about any important change.

Terms of Use

Gigables has zero tolerance for abusive users and objectionable content. Breaking these terms can get your content removed and your profile banned, without warning in serious cases.

These terms apply to usernames, friends, bands and shared gigs, and they add to Apple's Standard End User License Agreement. You accept them when you create a username. They are between you and Teo Kolić ("we"), not Apple.

Who can use these features

You must be at least 16 and signed in to iCloud. You may not create a new profile to get around a ban.

Your username

  • It must not impersonate anyone or suggest you are someone you are not.
  • It must not be offensive, or use a name or brand you have no right to use.
  • We can remove or reclaim a username that breaks these rules.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, bullying or hate towards anyone.
  • Sexual, violent, or otherwise objectionable content.
  • Spam, or repeated unwanted friend requests or invitations.
  • Fake gigs, scams, or misleading other people about a gig.
  • Sharing someone's personal details without their permission.
  • Modifying the app, or bypassing its limits, blocks or bans.
  • Anything illegal.

Your content

What you enter stays yours. You allow the people you share a gig or band with to see and keep that information, because that is how sharing works.

Shared gigs are provided by the organiser. Check important details with them, because we do not verify gigs and cannot guarantee an update reaches everyone in time.

Reporting and blocking

You can report or block any user in the app, or email gigables.support@gmail.com. We aim to review every report within 48 hours.

If we act on your content or profile, we will tell you why where we can. To challenge a decision, reply to that message or email us.

Ending your use

You can delete your profile at any time in Settings. We can remove content, or suspend or ban a profile, when these terms are broken or the law requires it. Your own gigs stay on your device and in your iCloud.

The service

The social features are free, and they may change or stop at any time. They depend on Apple's iCloud, so they can be unavailable when iCloud is.

Gigables helps you keep records, but it is not accounting or tax advice. You are responsible for checking your own amounts.

Liability

The app is provided as it is, as far as the law allows. We are not liable for missed gigs, lost income or indirect losses caused by using the shared features. Nothing here limits your rights as a consumer, or liability that cannot legally be limited.

Law and changes
